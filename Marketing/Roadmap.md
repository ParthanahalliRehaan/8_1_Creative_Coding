## 🎯 Indie Game Marketing Roadmap

### I. Foundation
- **Core Identity**  
  - Define your hook: what makes your game unique?  
  - Create a short pitch (1–2 sentences).  
  - Build a press kit (screenshots, trailer, logo, description).

- **Target Audience**  
  - Identify where your players hang out (Reddit, Discord, TikTok, Steam forums).  
  - Tailor tone: casual memes vs. serious devlogs.

---

### II. Social Media Presence
- **Instagram / Youtube**  
  - Short clips of gameplay loops, quirky mechanics, or funny bugs.  
  - Use trending sounds and hashtags (#indiegames, #gamedev).  
  - Post consistently (2–3 times per week).

---

### III. Community Building
- **Discord Server**  
  - Invite early fans to join.  
  - Share dev updates, polls, and sneak peeks.  
  - Encourage fan art or feedback.

- **Devlogs (YouTube / itch.io /patreon)**  
  - Monthly updates showing progress.  
  - Builds trust and keeps hype alive.

---

### IV. Outreach
- **Press & Influencers**  
  - Send your press kit to streamers and YouTubers who cover indie titles.  
  - Focus on micro-influencers (1k–50k followers) — they’re more likely to respond.  
  - Offer demo keys for playtesting.

- **Events**  
  - Submit to indie showcases (Steam Next Fest, Indiecade).  
  - Participate in game jams for visibility.

---

### V. Iteration
- Track which posts get the most engagement.  
- Double down on formats that resonate (memes, devlogs, trailers).  
- Adjust based on feedback — marketing is as iterative as game design.
