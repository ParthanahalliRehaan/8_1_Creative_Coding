## Alpha, Stable, Night category of LMMS!
**LMMS = Linux MultiMedia Studio** (now officially just **LMMS**).
- Install the **Stable** version, not Nightly or Alpha.
- **Stable:** Tested, reliable release for normal use.
- **Nightly:** Latest development build, may contain bugs or unfinished features.
- **Alpha:** Early experimental version, likely to have bugs and missing features.
