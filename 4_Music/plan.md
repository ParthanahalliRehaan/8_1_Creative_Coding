## GAME MUSIC & SOUND DESIGN MASTERY (20 days, self-paced)

**Philosophy:** You are the composer AND sound designer. Every track and SFX is made by you. No reliance on asset packs.

**Tools:**
- **FL Studio** (Windows) / **Logic Pro** (Mac) / **LMMS** (Free, all platforms)
- **Bfxr** or **sfxr** (free SFX generator)
- **FamiTracker** or **DefleMask** (chiptune, optional)
- **Audacity** (free audio editing)

---

### Phase 1: Music Theory & DAW Basics (5 days)

```
── Day 1 — DAW Interface & Basics
    ├── Interface:
    │   ├── Playlist/arrangement view
    │   ├── Piano roll / step sequencer
    │   ├── Mixer: channels, volume, pan
    │   ├── Browser: plugins, samples, presets
    │   └── Transport: play, stop, record, tempo, time signature
    │
    ├── First Track:
    │   ├── Create a simple 4-bar loop
    │   ├── Add a kick drum on every beat
    │   ├── Add a hi-hat on every off-beat
    │   ├── Play with tempo (80-140 BPM)
    │   └── Export as WAV
    │
    └── SKIP: Advanced mixing, mastering chains, sidechain compression

── Day 2 — Scales, Chords & Progressions
    ├── Music Theory:
    │   ├── Notes: C, D, E, F, G, A, B
    │   ├── Major scale formula: W-W-H-W-W-W-H
    │   ├── Minor scale formula: W-H-W-W-H-W-W
    │   ├── Chords: triads (1-3-5), inversions
    │   ├── Common progressions: I-V-vi-IV, ii-V-I, i-VI-III-VII
    │   └── Circle of fifths (conceptual)
    │
    ├── Practice:
    │   ├── Write a chord progression in C Major
    │   ├── Write a chord progression in A Minor
    │   ├── Arpeggiate each chord (broken chords)
    │   ├── Add a bass note following root of each chord
    │   └── Export both progressions
    │
    └── SKIP: Jazz theory, modal interchange, polychords, atonal music

── Day 3 — Rhythm & Drum Patterns
    ├── Rhythm Basics:
    │   ├── Time signatures: 4/4, 3/4, 6/8
    │   ├── Note values: whole, half, quarter, eighth, sixteenth
    │   ├── Rests and syncopation
    │   ├── Swing vs straight feel
    │   └── Tempo ranges by genre
    │
    ├── Drum Programming:
    │   ├── Four-on-the-floor (electronic)
    │   ├── Breakbeat patterns (hip-hop)
    │   ├── Rock beat: kick-snare-kick-kick-snare
    │   ├── Fill patterns: tom rolls, snare rolls
    │   ├── Layering: kick + sub-bass, snare + clap
    │   └── Humanization: slight timing/variation offsets
    │
    └── SKIP: Complex polyrhythms, odd time signatures, live drum recording

── Day 4 — Melody Writing
    ├── Melody Techniques:
    │   ├── Scale degrees: tonic, dominant, leading tone
    │   ├── Motif: short musical idea (2-4 notes)
    │   ├── Repetition + variation: A-A'-B-A"
    │   ├── Call and response
    │   ├── Stepwise motion vs leaps
    │   ├── Contour: rising, falling, arch-shaped
    │   └── Range: stay within an octave for game music
    │
    ├── Practice:
    │   ├── Write a 4-note motif
    │   ├── Repeat it with slight variation
    │   ├── Add a contrasting B section
    │   ├── Return to A with embellishment
    │   ├── Fit melody over your Day 2 chord progression
    │   └── Export melody + chords + drums
    │
    └── SKIP: Counterpoint, fugue, complex polyphony, atonal melodies

── Day 5 — Sound Selection & Instrumentation
    ├── Sound Design Basics:
    │   ├── Synth parameters: oscillator, filter, envelope (ADSR), LFO
    │   ├── Preset browsing: leads, pads, basses, plucks
    │   ├── Layering sounds for thickness
    │   ├── Frequency separation: bass/low, mids, highs
    │   └── Game music instrumentation:
    │       ├── Lead: melody (synth, flute, violin)
    │       ├── Pad: background harmony (strings, choir, synth pad)
    │       ├── Bass: low end foundation (bass synth, bass guitar)
    │       ├── Drums: rhythm (electronic kit, acoustic kit)
    │       └── Arp/Pluck: rhythmic texture (synth arpeggio, guitar)
    │   └── Mixing basics: volume balance, panning, EQ (cut, not boost)
    │
    └── SKIP: Advanced synthesis (FM, granular), complex mixing, mastering
```

### Phase 2: Game Music Genres & Composition (5 days)

```
── Day 6 — Chiptune / 8-Bit Music
    ├── Chiptune Basics:
    │   ├── What is chiptune: square, triangle, noise, pulse waves
    │   ├── Tools: FamiTracker, DefleMask, or chiptune VSTs
    │   ├── Limitations: 3-4 channels, no effects
    │   ├── Arpeggios: rapid chord notes for harmony
    │   ├── Duty cycle: pulse width for timbre variation
    │   └── Noise channel for percussion
    │
    ├── Composition:
    │   ├── Write a 30-second loop
    │   ├── Square wave lead melody
    │   ├── Triangle wave bass
    │   ├── Noise percussion
    │   ├── Arpeggiated chords on pulse channel
    │   └── Export as WAV + NSF (if using FamiTracker)
    │
    └── SKIP: Advanced tracker commands, DPCM samples, expansion chips

── Day 7 — Ambient / Atmospheric Music
    ├── Ambient Techniques:
    │   ├── Long, evolving pads
    │   ├── Minimal percussion or none
    │   ├── Reverb-heavy (large space feel)
    │   ├── Slow chord changes (every 4-8 bars)
    │   ├── Field recordings as texture (rain, wind)
    │   └── Layered drones for tension
    │
    ├── Composition:
    │   ├── 60-second ambient loop
    │   ├── 2-3 pad layers with slow filter sweeps
    │   ├── Sparse piano or bell notes
    │   ├── Heavy reverb and delay
    │   ├── Subtle volume automation
    │   └── Export as seamless loop
    │
    └── SKIP: Generative ambient, algorithmic composition, granular synthesis

── Day 8 — Action / Battle Music
    ├── Action Techniques:
    │   ├── Fast tempo (140-180 BPM)
    │   ├── Driving rhythm: syncopated bass + drums
    │   ├── Staccato melodies: short, punchy notes
    │   ├── Call and response: melody vs brass stabs
    │   ├── Build-ups: rising pitch, increasing density
    │   ├── Breakdowns: strip to drums + bass for tension
    │   └── Key change for intensity boost (up a semitone)
    │
    ├── Composition:
    │   ├── 45-second battle loop
    │   ├── Fast drums: double-time hi-hats
    │   ├── Aggressive bass line
    │   ├── Staccato string or synth lead
    │   ├── Brass stabs on off-beats
    │   └── Export as loop
    │
    └── SKIP: Orchestral mockups, live recording, complex arrangements

── Day 9 — Platformer / Adventure Music
    ├── Platformer Techniques:
    │   ├── Upbeat, catchy melodies (memorable = hummable)
    │   ├── Bouncy rhythm: syncopation, swing feel
    │   ├── Major key: happy, optimistic
    │   ├── Call and response: melody + echo
    │   ├── Layered arrangement: add instruments per section
    │   ├── Intro → A → B → A + drums → End
    │   └── Loop-friendly: end connects to start
    │
    ├── Composition:
    │   ├── 60-second platformer loop
    │   ├── Catchy 8-bar melody
    │   ├── Bouncy bass line following root notes
    │   ├── Light percussion: shaker, light kick
    │   ├── Layer addition: add instruments every 16 bars
    │   └── Export as seamless loop
    │
    └── SKIP: Complex orchestration, live instruments, dynamic music systems

── Day 10 — Menu / Title Screen Music
    ├── Menu Music Techniques:
    │   ├── Grand, establishing theme
    │   ├── Slower tempo than gameplay (80-100 BPM)
    │   ├── Full arrangement: all instruments
    │   ├── Build to a climax, then resolve
    │   ├── Memorable motif: this is your game's identity
    │   ├── Loop-friendly but with clear structure
    │   └── Emotional: sets the tone before gameplay
    │
    ├── Composition:
    │   ├── 45-second title theme
    │   ├── Intro: sparse, building
    │   ├── A section: main theme, full instrumentation
    │   ├── B section: contrasting, emotional
    │   ├── Return to A with added intensity
    │   └── Export as loop
    │
    └── SKIP: Full orchestral recording, choir, complex dynamic systems
```

### Phase 3: Sound Effects & Audio Implementation (5 days)

```
── Day 11 — SFX Theory & Tools
    ├── SFX Categories:
    │   ├── UI: click, hover, confirm, cancel, error
    │   ├── Player: jump, land, attack, hurt, death, pickup
    │   ├── Enemy: alert, attack, hurt, death
    │   ├── Environment: ambient loops, weather, machinery
    │   ├── Feedback: low health, combo, level up, achievement
    │   └── Foley: footsteps, cloth, impacts (often skipped in indie)
    │
    ├── Tools:
    │   ├── Bfxr / sfxr: retro 8-bit SFX generator
    │   ├── FL Studio: synthesize SFX from scratch
    │   ├── Audacity: record, edit, process
    │   ├── Layering: combine 2-3 sounds for richness
    │   └── Pitch variation: same sound, different pitch = variety
    │
    └── SKIP: Field recording, Foley artistry, complex SFX libraries

── Day 12 — Creating Core SFX
    ├── UI Sounds (Bfxr):
    │   ├── Hover: short blip, high pitch
    │   ├── Click: sharp tick, medium pitch
    │   ├── Confirm: pleasant chime, major third
    │   ├── Cancel: dull thud or descending tone
    │   ├── Error: buzz or dissonant beep
    │   └── Export each as WAV
    │
    ├── Player Sounds (synthesis):
    │   ├── Jump: quick pitch rise (saw wave)
    │   ├── Land: noise burst + low thud
    │   ├── Attack: swoosh (white noise filter sweep)
    │   ├── Hurt: descending tone + noise
    │   ├── Pickup: high chime, major scale arpeggio
    │   └── Death: descending glissando + noise fade
    │
    └── SKIP: Realistic Foley, 3D spatial audio, complex layering

── Day 13 — Godot Audio System
    ├── Godot Audio:
    │   ├── AudioStreamPlayer: general SFX
    │   ├── AudioStreamPlayer2D: positional SFX
    │   ├── Audio buses: Master, Music, SFX, UI
    │   ├── Bus effects: reverb, EQ, limiter
    │   ├── Volume control: AudioServer.set_bus_volume_db()
    │   ├── Pitch shifting: pitch_scale for variety
    │   └── Looping: seamless music loops
    │
    ├── Implementation:
    │   ├── Import WAV/OGG files
    │   ├── Set loop points for music
    │   ├── Create Audio bus layout
    │   ├── Add bus effects (reverb on SFX bus)
    │   ├── Volume slider connected to bus
    │   └── Test: play SFX on events, music on scene start
    │
    └── SKIP: 3D audio, dynamic mixing, adaptive music systems

── Day 14 — Music Looping & Transitions
    ├── Loop Techniques:
    │   ├── Seamless loops: start = end (crossfade or match)
    │   ├── Loop points: set in DAW or Godot
    │   ├── Tail handling: reverb/delay must loop cleanly
    │   ├── Export: render loop + tail separately if needed
    │   └── Test: play loop for 5 minutes, listen for clicks/pops
    │
    ├── Transitions:
    │   ├── Hard cut: immediate switch (jarring)
    │   ├── Crossfade: fade out A, fade in B (2-4 seconds)
    │   ├── Stinger: short musical phrase bridges A to B
    │   ├── Vertical layering: mute/unmute tracks (same tempo)
    │   └── Godot: use Tween for volume crossfade
    │
    └── SKIP: Horizontal resequencing, stem-based dynamic music

── Day 15 — Mixing & Loudness for Games
    ├── Mixing Basics:
    │   ├── Volume balance: music quieter than SFX (-12dB vs -6dB)
    │   ├── Frequency separation: EQ cuts to avoid mud
    │   ├── Sidechain compression: duck music when SFX plays
    │   ├── Limiting: prevent clipping (max -1dB)
    │   └── Reference: compare to similar games
    │
    ├── Loudness Standards:
    │   ├── LUFS: integrated loudness target (-14 to -16 LUFS)
    │   ├── Peak levels: never above -1dB
    │   ├── Dynamic range: don't over-compress
    │   ├── Platform differences: mobile vs PC vs console
    │   └── Export: 44.1kHz/48kHz, 16-bit, stereo
    │
    └── SKIP: Mastering for streaming, vinyl mastering, surround sound
```

### Phase 4: Adaptive Music & Advanced Techniques (5 days)

```
── Day 16 — Vertical Layering (Intensity System)
    ├── Concept:
    │   ├── Same tempo, same key, same structure
    │   ├── Layer 1: ambient/base (always playing)
    │   ├── Layer 2: percussion (combat active)
    │   ├── Layer 3: melody + harmony (exploration)
    │   ├── Layer 4: intensity (boss fight)
    │   └── Godot: mute/unmute AudioStreamPlayers per layer
    │
    ├── Implementation:
    │   ├── Compose 4 layers that fit together
    │   ├── Export each as separate OGG
    │   ├── Sync start: play all at once, mute unused
    │   ├── Crossfade layers: Tween volume over 2 seconds
    │   └── Test: switch layers smoothly in gameplay
    │
    └── SKIP: Complex stem systems, middleware (FMOD, Wwise)

── Day 17 — Stingers & Musical Cues
    ├── Stingers:
    │   ├── Short musical phrases (1-2 seconds)
    │   ├── Play over current music (no stop)
    │   ├── Uses: level up, power-up, combo milestone, victory
    │   ├── Same key as background music
    │   ├── Same instrumentation style
    │   └── Export: include tail/reverb
    │
    ├── Musical Cues:
    │   ├── Boss entrance: dramatic chord
    │   ├── Low health: heartbeat + tense strings
    │   ├── Time running out: ticking + rising pitch
    │   ├── Victory: major chord + flourish
    │   └── Defeat: minor chord + fade
    │
    └── SKIP: Complex cue systems, interactive scoring

── Day 18 — Sound Design Deep Dive
    ├── Synthesis for SFX:
    │   ├── Subtractive: oscillator → filter → envelope (ADSR)
    │   ├── FM synthesis: complex tones, bells, metals
    │   ├── Noise: explosions, wind, impacts
    │   ├── Sample manipulation: reverse, stretch, pitch
    │   └── Layering: kick + noise = punchy impact
    │
    ├── Advanced SFX:
    │   ├── Footsteps: 3-4 variations, pitch shift, random
    │   ├── Weapon swings: whoosh (filtered noise sweep)
    │   ├── Magic spells: chime + shimmer + reverb tail
    │   ├── Explosions: noise burst + low boom + debris
    │   └── Ambient loops: layered drones, subtle variation
    │
    └── SKIP: Procedural audio, physical modeling, convolution reverb

── Day 19 — Music for Different Game Moments
    ├── Compose for Scenarios:
    │   ├── Title screen: grand, memorable (Day 10)
    │   ├── Main menu: calm, inviting
    │   ├── Level 1: upbeat, tutorial feel
    │   ├── Level 2: slightly more complex, new instrument
    │   ├── Boss fight: intense, layered (Day 8)
    │   ├── Victory: triumphant, short (5 seconds)
    │   ├── Game over: somber, resolve
    │   └── Credits: reprise of title theme, full arrangement
    │
    ├── Consistency:
    │   ├── Same key or related keys across tracks
    │   ├── Shared motifs: title theme appears in level music
    │   ├── Same instrumentation palette
    │   ├── Tempo relationships: menu slower, gameplay faster
    │   └── Export all as OGG loops
    │
    └── SKIP: Full OST production, live orchestra, licensing

── Day 20 — Final Audio Pass & Integration
    ├── Polish:
    │   ├── Play through entire game with audio
    │   ├── Note moments where audio feels wrong
    │   ├── Adjust mix: SFX too loud? Music too quiet?
    │   ├── Add missing SFX: every action needs feedback
    │   ├── Remove redundant SFX: don't overdo it
    │   ├── Test loops: no clicks, seamless
    │   └── Test on different speakers: headphones, laptop, phone
    │
    ├── Documentation:
    │   ├── List all audio files
    │   ├── Note BPM and key for each track
    │   ├── SFX inventory: what plays when
    │   ├── Bus layout diagram
    │   └── Volume settings: default levels
    │
    └── SKIP: Audio middleware, complex dynamic systems, Dolby Atmos
```
