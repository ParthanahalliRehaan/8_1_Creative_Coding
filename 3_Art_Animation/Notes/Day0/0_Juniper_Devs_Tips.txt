🎨 Pixel Art Workflow (Libresprite), Dont have too many shades(not more than 3), Can use references, pinterest

Palette:
- Visit https://www.pixilart.com/palettes or https://lospec.com/palette-list
- Save any palette as PNG
- Load it in Libresprite
- Remember: use 3 shades per colour (shadow, base, highlight)

Outline:
- Draw a square box and trim, or draw outlines(Literally!)
- Enable "Pixel Perfect" in the pencil tool to remove doubles/jagged edges
- Reference existing sprites for smoother outlines
- Remove jaggedness?, look at ./Removing_Jagginess.png
- After all the shading and highlight is done, change the color of outline, black isnt compulsory
- Only the mobile object needs a outline, as we want it to stand out compared to ground

Fill:
- Fill the shape with your chosen base colour

Shading:
- Add shadow (darker than base) and highlight (lighter than base)
- Avoid pillow shading unless stylistically intended
- Fix a single light source for the entire game and stay consistent
- Add dithering, look at ./dithering.png
- Add Anti-aliasing, look at ./Anti-aliasing.png

Art Styling:
- Based on the lightning, adjust the outline so that the areas facing the light appear lighter, while the areas away from the light become darker

Tips:
- Shadows = darker tone of base
- Highlights = brightest tone of base
- Consistency in light direction keeps sprites believable
- continously, zoom in or out in preview window
